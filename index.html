What is SEO — and Why It Matters

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) simply means making your website easier to find on Google. When someone searches for "best pizza near me," SEO helps your restaurant show up in those results — instead of being buried on page 5.

A well-optimized site means more clicks, more visibility, and more customers — all without spending on ads. It's like having your restaurant's signpost placed right on the busiest street online.
