AUTHENTIC COMFORT FOOD

Organic & Fresh, just like Home

Authentic Flavours of Home

At My Kitchen, we serve family-style comfort food made with care and quality ingredients. We use organic chicken, grass-fed beef, organic eggs, non-GMO organic tofu, and always choose organic produce when possible. Our soups and stews are made from scratch daily using grass-fed beef bones and whole organic chickens for rich, hearty flavors. We are a locally owned, family-run restaurant — come visit us and enjoy the taste of home.
